About Us

A Message from the Principal

Mr. Ngetich Kipsang Bonde – Principal, Olkokwe Senior School

It gives me great pleasure to welcome you to Olkokwe Senior School. Our school is dedicated to providing quality and holistic education that prepares learners to become knowledgeable, skilled, disciplined and responsible members of society.

At Olkokwe Senior School, we believe that every learner possesses unique abilities and potential. Through effective teaching, mentorship, discipline, co-curricular activities and strong partnerships with parents and the community, we seek to nurture these abilities and enable every learner to thrive.

We are committed to creating a safe, inclusive and supportive environment where learners can pursue academic excellence, develop their talents and acquire values that will guide them throughout their lives.

I invite you to explore our website and discover what makes Olkokwe Senior School a place where education truly becomes power.

— Mr. Ngetich Kipsang Bonde, Principal
